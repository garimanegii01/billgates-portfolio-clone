About Me

I’m Bill Gates, a technologist, entrepreneur, and philanthropist. I co-founded Microsoft to help make personal computing accessible and practical for people around the world. Today, my work focuses on philanthropy through the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, where I concentrate on global health, education, and climate change. I believe thoughtful innovation and long-term thinking can help solve some of the world’s most important challenges.

Experience

  • Founder, Microsoft (1975–) — Co‑founded Microsoft and led development of Windows and Office, scaling the company into a global leader in software.
  • CEO & Chair (early leadership roles) — Drove product strategy, licensing, and partnerships that enabled mass adoption and industry standards.
  • Co‑Chair, Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation — Directed global grantmaking to improve health, education, and reduce poverty.
  • Philanthropist & Program Lead — Launched and funded large-scale initiatives in vaccines, infectious disease eradication, and education reform.
  • Author & Speaker — Wrote on technology and global challenges; frequent keynote speaker at industry and policy forums.
  • Education — Studied at Harvard University; recipient of multiple honorary degrees.
